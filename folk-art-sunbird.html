Folk Art Sunbird

A hand-drawn folk art sunbird, illustrated in Adobe Illustrator and is brought to life with layered SVG and CSS animation — the sun's rays spin slowly, the wings flap independently, and the head bobs gently.

Process

Each part of the illustration, the rays, the sun's face, the wings, the head and body was drawn and named as a separate layer in Illustrator before exporting.So each piece could be targeted individually once the file became SVG. From there, small CSS keyframe animations were layered onto each part: a slow constant rotation for the rays around the sun's center, an alternating wing flap timed slightly apart across the two visible wings and the back wing for a sense of depth, and a subtle head bob to keep the piece feeling alive rather than mechanical.

Tools

Adobe Illustrator · SVG · CSS animation
